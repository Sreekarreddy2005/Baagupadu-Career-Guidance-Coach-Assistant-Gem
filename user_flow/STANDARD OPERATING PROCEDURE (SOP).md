# 1.0 PURPOSE

The purpose of this Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) is to establish a structured daily workflow for all interns. It defines the mandatory schedule, specific roles and responsibilities, and technological restrictions to ensure maximum productivity, transparency, and accountability.

This document also serves as the functional requirement specification for the **Internal Monitoring Application (Agentic AI System)** being developed to automate timekeeping and compliance enforcement.

---

# 2.0 SCOPE

This SOP applies to all interns currently enrolled in the organization's internship program. It governs:

- Daily login/logout procedures
- Work schedule adherence
- Break and lunch compliance
- Document upload restrictions
- Timekeeping and attendance tracking

This policy is applicable during regular working days and does not extend to weekends, public holidays, or approved leave periods.

---

# 3.0 DEFINITIONS & TERMINOLOGY

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Agentic AI** | An autonomous artificial intelligence system that monitors, enforces, and automates compliance with workflow policies without manual intervention. |
| **Timekeeping** | The process of recording employee work hours, including clock-in and clock-out times, break periods, and total hours worked. |
| **System Lockout** | An automated restriction preventing user access to work systems during designated non-work periods. |
| **Access Restriction** | The disabling of specific system functionalities (e.g., file uploads) during designated time windows. |
| **Mandatory 9-Hour Policy** | The requirement that every intern must remain logged in for a total of 9 hours per day, inclusive of all scheduled breaks. |
| **OneDrive** | The organization's cloud-based document storage and file-sharing platform. |

# 4.0 ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Intern** | - Adhere strictly to the daily schedule.<br>- Complete all documentation before **10:45 AM**.<br>- Maintain **100% attendance** during mentor-led classes.<br>- Comply with all access restrictions and lockout policies. |

# 5.0 PROCEDURE

## 5.1 Daily Workflow Schedule (Mandatory Timelines)

The following schedule is strictly enforced. The Agentic AI system will track all logins, logouts, and activity periods to ensure compliance with the **Mandatory 9-Hour Policy**.

| Time Slot | Duration | Activity | Key Deliverables / Compliance Notes |
|-----------|----------|----------|--------------------------------------|
| **09:30 – 10:45** | 75 mins | System Login & Documentation | - Log in to the internal dashboard.<br>- Document video recordings from Chayan Sir's sessions.<br>- Ensure all transcripts/notes are saved to OneDrive before the upload restriction activates. |
| **10:45 – 11:00** | 15 mins | Short Break | Sanctioned rest period. System remains active but idle. |
| **11:00 – 13:20** | 140 mins | Mentor-led Classes | Active participation required. No background tasks permitted. |
| **13:20 – 14:20** | 60 mins | Lunch Break (Strict) | Strict no-work zone. System Lockout activates automatically. |
| **14:20 – 15:45** | 85 mins | Project Work | Focused execution of assigned project deliverables. |
| **15:45 – 16:00** | 15 mins | Short Break | Sanctioned rest period. |
| **16:00 – 18:00** | 120 mins | Upskilling / LinkedIn / Doubt Clearance | - Self-paced learning modules.<br>- Professional profile maintenance.<br>- Live query resolution with mentors. |

---

## 5.2 Mandatory 9-Hour Work Policy

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Total Required Time** | 9 hours per day, inclusive of breaks. |
| **Calculation Method** | Total time from **First Login** to **Last Logout**. |
| **Late Login** | If an intern logs in after **09:30**, they must log out correspondingly later to meet the 9-hour quota. |
| **Non-Compliance** | Failure to complete 9 hours will result in an **Incomplete Working Hours** flag in the attendance log. |

---

## 5.3 OneDrive Upload Restriction (The 11:00 AM Rule)

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Restriction Time** | Daily at **11:00 AM** sharp. |
| **Action** | Upload functionality to OneDrive is automatically disabled by the Agentic AI system. |
| **Rationale** | To prevent distractions during high-focus mentor classes and ensure all administrative documentation is completed during the first slot (**09:30 – 10:45**). |
| **Penalty** | Attempting to bypass this restriction will trigger an automatic warning notification to the HR/Administration team. |

---

## 5.4 Strict Lunch Break Rule

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Restriction Time** | **13:20 – 14:20** daily. |
| **Action** | No work is permitted during this window. |
| **System Enforcement** | - Interns must log out of all work-related systems.<br>- The Internal Monitoring App displays a lock screen or **Break Mode**.<br>- The timer stops counting toward the 9-hour requirement until **14:20**. |
| **Penalty** | Working during lunch break is a policy violation and will be recorded in the compliance log. |

# 6.0 TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS: INTERNAL MONITORING APPLICATION

The organization requires an **Agentic AI-powered application** to monitor these workflows. The system must include the following features.

## 6.1 Timekeeping & Monitoring

- **Real-time Timer:** Tracks total logged-in time per user.
- **Break Alerts:** Automatically locks the system during lunch breaks (**13:20 – 14:20**).
- **Late Login Compensation:** If a user logs in at **09:40**, the system must display a warning that they must log out at **18:10** to complete the 9-hour policy.

## 6.2 Automation (Agentic AI)

- **Upload Control:** An automated AI agent must execute a script to disable OneDrive upload functionality precisely at **11:00 AM** daily.
- **Activity Validation:** AI should monitor for inactive periods during Mentor Classes to ensure interns are present.
- **Compliance Reporting:** Generate daily reports on attendance, break compliance, and upload restriction adherence.
